About

I am a Senior Machine Learning Scientist in the Data, AI & Genome Sciences Department at Merck Research Laboratories.

My research on Bayesian transfer learning received an AISTATS Spotlight presentation (top 2.5% of submissions). I was selected as a recipient of the 2026 Outstanding Academic Scholarship Award from the Tufts University School of Engineering.

I graduated with an M.S. and Ph.D. in Computer Science from Tufts University, where I was a member of the Machine Learning Research Group advised by Michael C. Hughes. Prior to Tufts, I graduated with a B.S. in Computer Science and Mathematics minor from Grove City College, where I did research with Jonathan O. Hutchins.
